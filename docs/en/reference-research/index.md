# Reference & Research

This section is the research-facing half of the docs. It is not here to inflate the project with name-dropping. It is here to provide a research agenda for how to compare the repository to adjacent work.

## Research agenda

A useful research agenda for this codebase has three parts:

1. **related projects** — what neighboring systems solve and what they imply for deployment expectations,
2. **references** — which papers explain the math, compiler model, or performance framing,
3. **evolution thinking** — what an industrial, evidence-backed kernel library should keep improving without losing focus.
